# ForgeTrace Community Conduct

ForgeTrace is an open-source project created by **Rooke Poole**. Contributors, reviewers, maintainers, and users are expected to help build a technically rigorous and welcoming project.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, evidence-based feedback.
- Credit implementation, testing, design, documentation, review, issue discovery, and support work.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person’s repository contents, credentials, or personal information without permission.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a technical or governance decision.
- Accept corrections and update claims when evidence changes.
- Keep security reports private until a safe disclosure is coordinated.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, or deliberate humiliation.
- Plagiarism or removal of required license and attribution notices.
- Publishing secrets, private files, or exploit details irresponsibly.
- Manipulating contribution records or knowingly presenting inferred credit as verified fact.
- Repeatedly disrupting technical work after maintainers have set a clear boundary.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation channels, or restrict contributors when necessary to protect the project and its participants. Decisions should be documented proportionately and may be reconsidered when new evidence is available.
